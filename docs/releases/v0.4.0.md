Skylauncher 0.4.0 adds Skylanders: Giants. Install it from your own disc and play it, with the same figures as Spyro's Adventure.

## What's new
- **Giants** installs and plays from your own disc image, like Spyro's Adventure: 30 fps steady, with a 60 fps option, widescreen and ultrawide. Each game has its own page, colours and settings.
- **A new Toy Box and Collection.** The same screens in the launcher and in the game: the portal's places on top, your figures below. Pick a place, then a figure.
- **One figure collection** for both games. A figure keeps its level, gold and hats from one game to the other; Spyro's Adventure shows it at its own maximum level without changing it.
- **Every variant the games know**, read from your disc: Series 2, LightCore, Legendary and alternate colours, with Giants' large artworks.
- **Upgrades by name.** Edit a figure's upgrades with their real names, prices and paths, as the game shows them.
- **Faster installs.** Spyro's Adventure takes about 10 minutes, Giants about 15.
- **Copy the game data** (Disc tab): after that you can play without the disc image.
- **French.** The launcher follows your system language, or you can choose one in Settings.
- **Shorter texts, clearer states.** The sidebar, Home and the main button show each game's state, with install progress. Messages appear at the top right.
- The keyboard and gamepad reach every control, and the launcher no longer redraws while idle.

## Install
1. Download Skylauncher for your system below.
2. Drop your own disc image on the window.
3. Click INSTALL, then PLAY.

## Known issues
- Giants' voice language cannot be changed yet.
- Giants on Windows and Linux has not been tested on a real PC yet. Spyro's Adventure runs on both.
- The Mac app is not notarized: the first time, right-click it and choose Open. On Windows, SmartScreen may warn you: choose More info, then Run anyway.
