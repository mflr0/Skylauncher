Skylauncher is now **Arkeyan Launcher**, and it adds Skylanders: Swap Force. Your games, settings, collection and desktop shortcuts carry over: nothing to do.

## What's new
- **Swap Force** installs and plays from your own disc image, like the other games: the 3D at your screen's full resolution (or the console's size, in Settings), 30 fps with a 60 fps option, widescreen and ultrawide.
- **Swappers in the Collection and the Toy Box.** One card per swapper, with its top and bottom pictures together. A mixed swapper is named the way the game names it.
- **Game updates.** When a game has a newer version, the main button says UPDATE; you can still play without updating. From now on an update only rebuilds what changed; the first one, to this version, rebuilds the whole game once.
- **Search and filters** in the Collection.
- **Uninstall everything**, in Settings. Your saves and figures stay unless you tick them.
- **Button icons follow what you play with**, in Giants' world and Swap Force's texts. On a keyboard they show the keys you chose.
- The games have their own icon on Windows and Linux.
- The game kit is now `skylanders-kit-<platform>.zip`, with `SkyBuild` as its builder.

## The new name
- The window, the menus, the app and the files are called Arkeyan Launcher (`ArkeyanLauncher.exe`, `ArkeyanLauncher-macOS.dmg`, `ArkeyanLauncher-x86_64.AppImage`). The macOS bundle id is unchanged, so macOS does not ask for folder permissions again.
- A launcher you already have updates itself in place and keeps its file name (`Skylauncher.exe`, `Skylauncher.app`...). The release also carries the files under their old names, so that Skylauncher 0.4.x finds the update.
- Desktop shortcuts made by Skylauncher keep working; the launcher renames them at its next start.

## Fixes
- French texts fit their buttons.
- Pages scroll only up and down.
- A new figure no longer shows "Last played: Never".
- "Uninstall everything" works when a folder is reached through a link.

## Install
1. Download Arkeyan Launcher for your system below.
2. Drop your own disc image on the window.
3. Click INSTALL, then PLAY. If a game is already installed, Arkeyan Launcher offers to update it.

## Known issues
- Swap Force is new: not every mode has been played through yet.
- Swap Force on Windows and Linux has not been tested on a real PC yet.
- The Mac app is not notarized: the first time, right-click it and choose Open. On Windows, SmartScreen may warn you: choose More info, then Run anyway.
