Arkeyan Launcher 0.6.1 sets your keys per game, shows the right buttons for your controller, cleans up the Toy Box and fixes what testers found in 0.6.0.

## What's new
- **Keys per game.** Each game's settings have a Keyboard tab: every row says what the key does in that game, with the controller button in brackets ("Attack 1 (X)"). Keys keep their place on any keyboard: on an AZERTY keyboard the default movement shows and works as Z Q S D.
- **The right buttons for your controller.** The games show Xbox 360, Xbox One / Series, PlayStation or Switch buttons for the controller you play with, and on the keyboard the keys you chose for that game.
- **The Toy Box.** One entry per Skylander (no more "Standard" next to it), each game shows only the figures it can use, and **On the wheel** puts your own Skylanders on the quick wheel (hold Tab). The Collection gets a "Works in" filter.
- **Swappers** are shown whole, top and bottom joined at the waist.
- **Each game's icon** is its own logo, in the Dock, on the taskbar and on desktop shortcuts.
- **The launcher's language** follows your system, or the one you choose; it no longer changes with the game shown.
- **A clearer update screen**, shorter texts and many small visual fixes.

## The games (they update from the launcher)
- **Spyro's Adventure 0.9.2, Giants 0.8.2, Swap Force 0.5.1.**
- The settings you pick (skip the intro movies, 60 FPS...) apply however you start the game, from the launcher or from its icon.
- At 60 FPS, a computer that cannot keep up shows a steady 30 FPS with a short note, instead of stuttering.
- Giants and Swap Force load about twice as fast.
- Button prompts follow your controller or keyboard everywhere, including Giants' rock prompt and Swap Force's title screen.

## Fixes
- "Couldn't save the settings" on the very first start on a new PC.
- Windows user names with accents.
- The Collection (issue #9): Edit stats shows each limit, a seat is named once, a seat or `launcher.ini` is never left half written, a portal place that is gone is ignored, and a `launcher.ini` saved with a byte order mark is read.

## Updating
- From Arkeyan Launcher 0.6.0: it updates itself, then offers to update each game.
- From Skylauncher 0.5 or older: it will not find this release. Download Arkeyan Launcher for your system below; your games, settings, collection and shortcuts carry over.

## Install
1. Download Arkeyan Launcher for your system below.
2. Drop your own disc image on the window.
3. Click INSTALL, then PLAY.

## Known issues
- Giants and Swap Force on Windows and Linux have not been tested on a real PC yet.
- 60 FPS at the highest resolution scales is not held everywhere yet: that is the next release's work.
- The Mac app is not notarized: the first time, right-click it and choose Open. On Windows, SmartScreen may warn you: choose More info, then Run anyway.
