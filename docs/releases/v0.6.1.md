Arkeyan Launcher 0.6.1 fixes the Collection, redraws the update screen and joins the swappers' pictures.

## What's new
- **A clearer update screen.** The game's page says what the update is (the versions, the download, the time), and **Play without updating** sits right under that line. The band at the bottom shows only the portal line under the main button, as for PLAY.
- **Swappers' pictures joined at the waist.** In the Collection and the Toy Box the top and the bottom of a swapper now meet, at one scale, instead of showing two separate pictures. For the Toy Box, Swap Force updates to 0.5.1.
- **Release files.** A release carries the files under the Arkeyan Launcher names only: `ArkeyanLauncher*`, `skylanders-kit-<platform>.zip` and `SHA256SUMS`.

## Fixes
- The Collection (issue #9): Edit stats shows each limit, a seat is named once, a seat or `launcher.ini` is never left half written, a portal place that is gone is ignored, and a `launcher.ini` saved with a byte order mark is read.

## Updating
- From Arkeyan Launcher 0.6.0: it updates itself.
- From Skylauncher 0.5 or older: it will not find this release. Download Arkeyan Launcher for your system below; your games, settings, collection and shortcuts carry over.

## Install
1. Download Arkeyan Launcher for your system below.
2. Drop your own disc image on the window.
3. Click INSTALL, then PLAY.

## Known issues
- Swap Force on Windows and Linux has not been tested on a real PC yet.
- The Mac app is not notarized: the first time, right-click it and choose Open. On Windows, SmartScreen may warn you: choose More info, then Run anyway.
